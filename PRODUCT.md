# Product brief

Problem: Keep emergency checklist and contacts available during an outage.

Audience: families and caregivers

## First release

One user role, one core record (checklists) and one reversible workflow. Delay integrations and notifications until a backlog item justifies them.

## Guardrail

Sensitive location and contact data need local protection, recovery and safe sharing.

Measure task completion, correction rate, offline recovery, trust, retention and deletion only after purpose and consent are documented.
